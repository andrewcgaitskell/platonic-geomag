
Classic Series (Standard / Regular Rods)

• Rod Length (Caliper length): 27.00 mm (1.08 in)
• Rod Diameter: ~7.35 mm to 7.40 mm
• Rod Weight: 4.70 g (0.166 oz)
• Sphere / Ball Diameter: 12.70 mm (0.50 in, or a standard 1/2-inch steel ball bearing; note that spheres are non-magnetic steel)
• Ball Weight: 8.60 g (0.303 oz)
• Geometric Distance (center-to-center between spheres): 38.84 mm (1.530 in)
• Dimple Depth: 0.43 mm (0.017 in — how far the ball impinges past the end of the rod)

Pro-L Series (Longer / Stronger Rods)

• Rod Length: 58.0 mm (5.8 cm)
• Rod Diameter: ~7.35 mm
• Sphere / Ball Diameter: 12.70 mm (1.2 cm / 0.5 in, compatible with standard spheres)
• Note on Pro-L: Features stronger neodymium magnets and visible polarity markings on the rods.
